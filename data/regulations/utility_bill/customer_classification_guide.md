# Customer Classification Guide

## Residential

Single-family and multi-family dwelling units. Lowest per-kWh rate and no
grid-capacity surcharge, reflecting typical low-and-flat usage profiles.

## Commercial

Retail, office, and small business accounts. Moderate per-kWh rate, larger
free allowance than residential (reflecting minimum operating loads), no
surcharge.

## Industrial

Manufacturing, processing, and other high-draw accounts. Lowest per-kWh
rate but the largest free allowance and a grid-capacity surcharge, since
industrial demand profiles drive peak-capacity planning for the whole
distribution network.

## Misclassification

An account billed under the wrong customer class will show a total that
looks like a calculation error even though the arithmetic against the
*wrong* class's rate is entirely correct — the discrepancy is in which rate
row applied, not in the math applied to it.
