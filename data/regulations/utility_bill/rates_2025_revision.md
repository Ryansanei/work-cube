# Rate Schedule Revision — Effective 2025-07-01

## What changed

Service charges and per-kWh rates increased approximately 8% across all
customer classes, reflecting updated generation costs. Free allowances and
surcharge percentages are unchanged from the prior schedule.

## New: solar generation credit

Residential accounts with verified rooftop solar generation are eligible
for the SOLAR-CREDIT discount, introduced in this revision. It did not
exist under the prior schedule — a bill dated before 2025-07-01 claiming
this discount is claiming something that wasn't available yet on that date,
regardless of whether the account now qualifies.

## Existing discounts, unchanged

The LOW-INCOME discount (all customer classes) and the
COMMERCIAL-EFFICIENCY rebate (commercial accounts only, requires a verified
efficiency upgrade) carry over unchanged from the prior schedule.
