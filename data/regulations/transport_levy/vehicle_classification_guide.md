# Vehicle Classification Guide

The declared vehicle class is the second axis of the rate table, alongside
cargo class. It reflects the carrying vehicle's registered category, not the
weight actually carried on a given trip.

## Light Truck

Single-unit rigid vehicles with a registered gross vehicle weight under
7,500 kg. Typical of last-mile and regional distribution runs.

## Heavy Truck

Single-unit rigid vehicles with a registered gross vehicle weight of 7,500
kg or more. Typical of long-haul regional freight.

## Articulated Vehicle

Tractor-trailer combinations (a separate powered tractor unit and one or
more trailers). Articulated vehicles carry the highest base fee and per-km
rate of the three vehicle classes, reflecting their higher road-wear
footprint, but also the highest free weight threshold before the per-kg
charge applies — they are built to carry substantially more weight per trip
before incurring a weight charge.
