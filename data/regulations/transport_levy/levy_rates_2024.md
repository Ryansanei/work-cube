# Levy Rate Table — Original Schedule (effective 2024-01-01)

This notice sets out the base fee, per-kilometre rate, and per-kilogram
rate for each cargo/vehicle combination under the original levy schedule,
effective 2024-01-01 through 2025-06-30. Superseded by the 2025-07 revision
for shipments dated 2025-07-01 or later.

## General Freight

Light truck: base fee 40.00, 0.35/km, 0.008/kg over 2,000 kg. Heavy truck:
base fee 65.00, 0.55/km, 0.006/kg over 5,000 kg. Articulated: base fee
95.00, 0.75/km, 0.005/kg over 10,000 kg. No surcharge.

## Perishable Goods

Light truck: base fee 45.00, 0.40/km, 0.009/kg over 1,500 kg. Heavy truck:
base fee 72.00, 0.60/km, 0.007/kg over 4,000 kg. Articulated: base fee
105.00, 0.80/km, 0.006/kg over 9,000 kg. No surcharge.

## Hazardous Materials

Light truck: base fee 80.00, 0.70/km, 0.012/kg over 1,000 kg, 12.5%
surcharge. Heavy truck: base fee 130.00, 0.95/km, 0.010/kg over 3,000 kg,
12.5% surcharge. Articulated: base fee 190.00, 1.20/km, 0.009/kg over 8,000
kg, 15.0% surcharge.

## Oversized Loads

Light truck: base fee 70.00, 0.60/km, 0.010/kg over 1,500 kg, 5.0%
surcharge. Heavy truck: base fee 115.00, 0.85/km, 0.008/kg over 4,000 kg,
5.0% surcharge. Articulated: base fee 160.00, 1.05/km, 0.007/kg over 9,000
kg, 7.5% surcharge.
