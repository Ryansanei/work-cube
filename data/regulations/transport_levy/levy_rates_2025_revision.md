# Levy Schedule Revision 2025-07

Effective 2025-07-01, the NTLA revises the base fee, per-kilometre rate, and
per-kilogram rate across all cargo/vehicle combinations. Free weight
thresholds and surcharge percentages are unchanged from the original
schedule. This revision applies to shipments dated 2025-07-01 or later;
shipments dated before that remain under the original 2024 schedule.

## Summary of changes

Base fee, per-km rate, and per-kg rate each increase by approximately 8%
across every cargo/vehicle combination, reflecting infrastructure
maintenance cost increases. This revision also introduces the COLD-CHAIN
exemption for perishable cargo (see the exemptions notice) — no equivalent
exemption existed under the original schedule.

## Revised rate table

General freight — light truck: 43.20 base, 0.3780/km, 0.0086/kg. Heavy
truck: 70.20 base, 0.5940/km, 0.0065/kg. Articulated: 102.60 base,
0.8100/km, 0.0054/kg.

Perishable goods — light truck: 48.60 base, 0.4320/km, 0.0097/kg. Heavy
truck: 77.76 base, 0.6480/km, 0.0076/kg. Articulated: 113.40 base,
0.8640/km, 0.0065/kg.

Hazardous materials — light truck: 86.40 base, 0.7560/km, 0.0130/kg,
12.5% surcharge. Heavy truck: 140.40 base, 1.0260/km, 0.0108/kg, 12.5%
surcharge. Articulated: 205.20 base, 1.2960/km, 0.0097/kg, 15.0% surcharge.

Oversized loads — light truck: 75.60 base, 0.6480/km, 0.0108/kg, 5.0%
surcharge. Heavy truck: 124.20 base, 0.9180/km, 0.0086/kg, 5.0% surcharge.
Articulated: 172.80 base, 1.1340/km, 0.0076/kg, 7.5% surcharge.

Weight thresholds are unchanged from the original schedule for every
combination.
