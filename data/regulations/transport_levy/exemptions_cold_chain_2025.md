# COLD-CHAIN Exemption (introduced 2025-07-01)

## COLD-CHAIN

Introduced as part of the 2025-07 schedule revision to incentivize
temperature-controlled freight. Shipments correctly declaring perishable
cargo may apply the COLD-CHAIN code for a 15% discount on the subtotal.

This exemption did not exist under the original 2024 schedule — a
shipment dated before 2025-07-01 cannot claim it, regardless of cargo
class, since the code had not yet been introduced. Applies to perishable
cargo only; has no effect if declared against any other cargo class.
